# Code of Conduct

BEMBEL is a small project about a specific city. People come to it as
developers, as Wasserhäuschen regulars, as open-data civil servants and as
reviewers of AI-written code. This is the standard everyone is held to, in
the repository, in bembel-data, and anywhere the project is represented.

## The standard

- **Be kind to the person, hard on the change.** Review the diff, the data
  row, the argument. Not the author, their employer, their tooling or their
  German.
- **Assume competence, ask before assuming intent.** A confusing PR gets a
  question, not a verdict.
- **No harassment, full stop.** No slurs, no sexualised language or
  attention, no doxxing, no sustained hostility, no "jokes" that need the
  target to be a good sport. Publishing someone's private information —
  including a real name behind a GitHub handle — without their consent is
  harassment.
- **Facts about places, not about people.** The community register records
  what a kiosk offers and when it is open, never who works there beyond
  what they publish themselves. A rating is about a place.
- **Disagreement goes to the issue, decisions go to the ADR.** See
  [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Relitigating a locked decision in every
  thread is not persistence, it is noise, and it will be closed as such.
- **Agents are held to the same standard as people**, and the human who ran
  the agent is responsible for what it posted.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer,
[@maurice-jobst](https://github.com/maurice-jobst), by GitHub direct message
or through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/maurice-jobst/bembel/security/advisories/new)
(it is built for vulnerabilities, but it is private and it works). If the
concern is about the maintainer, contact one of the lane reviewers named in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#2-roles) instead. Expect an acknowledgement
within a week.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides, in proportion: a private word, a public correction,
a locked thread, a temporary or permanent block from the project's
repositories. The person reported is told what was decided and why. Reports
are kept confidential except as needed to act on them.

This document is adapted to the project's scale; it takes its structure and
spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
